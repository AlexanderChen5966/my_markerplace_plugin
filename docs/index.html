Watch a ticket 看一張票怎麼走

每張票依序走過需求、開發、審查、收尾；審查沒過會退回開發。最後的 commit、push、開 MR 一律由人處理。
Each ticket moves through requirements, build, review and wrap-up; a failed review sends it back to build. Committing, pushing and opening the MR are always done by a person.
